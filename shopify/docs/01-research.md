## 1.2 Top 10 patterns to adopt

| # | Pattern | Why it works | Best at it | Where it lives in our build |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Proof next to the CTA** [BRAND] | Trust is weighed at the moment of purchase, not in the footer (brand book 1.5, 9.2) | Seed ○ | Proof chips + "See the evidence" sit directly under the CTA |
| 2 | **Subscribe & save as a two-row choice with visible savings** [CMO] | Showing both prices together makes the saving concrete without shouting | Zesty Paws ✅ / Finn ✅ | Purchase option fieldset, teal "Save X%" tag |
| 3 | **Size cards that show price and value** [CMO] | Turns choosing a size into a value comparison, which lifts AOV | Chewy ○ | Size cards with unit price, "Save ₹X" and "Most popular" |
| 4 | **Dose by body weight, structured** [UX] | Answers "Am I doing this right?" before purchase (brand book 2.5) | Pooch & Mutt ◐ / Finn ✅ | Dosage table + weight calculator |
| 5 | **Pincode delivery check on the PDP** [CMO] | Indian shoppers expect it, and it reduces COD/RTO surprises | Supertails ✅ | Pincode form (needs an endpoint) |
| 6 | **Editorial whitespace, few elements, large imagery** [BRAND] | Restraint signals luxury (6.1) | Wild One ○ | Chalk canvas, hairlines, a 1440px grid |
| 7 | **Hero ingredients with amount, role and source** [BRAND] | Wins the "Label Reader" segment (2.2) | Ritual ○ / Seed ○ | "Why it works" ingredient list |
| 8 | **Price per day** [CMO] | Re-frames a premium price as a daily ritual | The Farmer's Dog ○ | `≈ ₹13 a day for a 10 kg dog` (needs approved basis) |
| 9 | **Need-led framing and one category colour** [BRAND] | Makes "Is this for my pet?" instantly answerable (4.4) | Pooch & Mutt ◐ / HOLY CAP ○ | Species + need chips; accent from the need metafield |
| 10 | **Sticky mobile purchase bar after the CTA scrolls away** [UX] | Keeps the action in reach without crowding the first view (9.7) | Standard across leaders ○ | `.vf-sticky`, docked above the footer |

## 1.3 Five anti-patterns to avoid

| # | Anti-pattern | Why it reads "cheap" or breaks trust | Seen at |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Promo stack above the fold**: % off pill + coupons + bank offers + countdown | Discount-first contradicts "editorial before promotional" (9.2) and lowers perceived value | Supertails ◐, Chewy ○, Petco ○ |
| 2 | **Badge confetti**: many unsubstantiated seals ("#1", "100% safe", "vet recommended") | Faux-medical seals are banned (8.3), and "vet recommended" is high-risk language (5.4) | Zesty Paws (badges) ○, marketplace sellers |
| 3 | **Everything in one description dump** | Hides dose and safety, and fails the scannability rule (6.8) | Most Indian D2C PDPs ○, our current v6.1 |
| 4 | **Dense spec tabs and Q&A walls on mobile** | Creates cognitive load on a phone; looks like a marketplace | Petco ○, Chewy ○ |
| 5 | **Neumorphic/glassy UI, pill buttons everywhere, saturated orange CTAs** | Ages fast, weakens hierarchy, retired in brand book 1.3 | Our current v6.1 |

## 1.4 Vettofit's differentiation (brand book 3.2)

Three points to hold:

- **Against Pooch & Mutt** [BRAND]: keep their breadth and need-led clarity. Win on editorial premium: a Chalk canvas, Helvetica set tight, one accent, and evidence shown as information rather than as badges. Pooch & Mutt sells "a supplement for X". Vettofit presents a vet-reviewed routine, with a named reviewer card, a dosage table and a timeline with a vet threshold.
- **Against Finn** [CMO]: keep the subscription mechanics and the approachable science. Finn is colourful and illustrated. Vettofit is quieter and more luxurious: photography instead of illustration, and Carbon CTAs instead of bright fills. Sell continuity (dose, timeline, ask a vet) rather than gamified bundles. Add a quiz only once one exists (Q7 default).
- **What only Vettofit does here** [BRAND]: the Night Plum evidence band, "What earns your trust", carries the proof signature *Vet-backed. Pet-first.* It turns Ch. 5 compliance into a design feature. Every claim in it is typed (vet-approved / human-grade / clinically studied / third-party tested for…) and dated.
